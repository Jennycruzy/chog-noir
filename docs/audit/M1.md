## Escrow and payment-window follow-up — 16 October

The full offline import stores all 180 verified escrow movements. The database accepts a deal advancing from creation to seller payment to collection. It rejects changed amounts and attempts to erase an existing payment. Refreshing classifications is an explicit `--reclassify` operation that preserves original chain records.

Public RPC checks: `debug_traceTransaction` with `callTracer` works on the saved historical transactions. `trace_transaction` and `trace_filter` return method-not-found errors. A zero-result response from an unavailable trace service is never treated as proof of no payment.

The Etherscan key works for Monad account histories. A direct-transfer window at transaction `0xe3fa036d…7824`, log 142, has all three histories checked: external MON, internal MON and official WMON transfers. No payment from receiver to sender was found within one hour before or after the transfer. The known MON escrow deposit `0xac5436c4…346e`, log 132, correctly returns payment found. The full replies and requests without credentials are saved as test fixtures.

These are limited findings about recorded payments. They do not establish that a holder intended a gift, identify a person, or rule out payment in another asset or outside the checked window.

[Payment-window evidence](evidence/M1-payment-windows.json) records both checked examples and their API fetch times. Positive account-history findings remain pending until matched to successful receipts and execution calls or official token logs.
